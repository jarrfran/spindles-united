Up the Spins

A Club for the Community.

Spindles United FC is an amateur soccer club based in Cohoes, New York. Our men’s side competes in the Upstate Premier League. We’re here to grow accessible, community-driven football in the Capital Region.

Beyond the Pitch

Built Around People.

Our community work has included youth sports camps, local partnerships and the Grassroots Cup. From 2023 through 2025, the club ran Cohoes Recreational Soccer’s Soccer Tots program for children ages four to six. That three-season program is part of our history; we no longer run it.

Our Story

From an Idea to a Club.

A Cohoes Beginning

Spindles began league play in 2022 with a simple aim: give the Capital Region a club people could identify with, build around and support. The early years were about assembling a team, finding local partners and establishing a place in the amateur game.

The second season brought a refreshed identity and a first CDSL final appearance. The new crest and red shirts gave the project a clearer voice, while the players and supporters gave it momentum.

More Than the Match

The club’s work has always extended beyond the final whistle. Players, volunteers, photographers, local businesses and supporters all help shape what Spindles becomes.

Our mission is to make grassroots football feel worth belonging to: competitive on the pitch, welcoming around it, and connected to the community that makes it possible.

Helping Build the Game

A Founding Voice in the UPL.

Spindles United helped form the Upstate Premier League alongside Collar City FC and Mohawk City FC. The league began play in 2026, creating a home-and-away competition centered on organized, community-based clubs.

That role brings responsibility as well as opportunity. The club has contributed to league operations and shares the UPL’s ambition to strengthen the standard, identity and long-term sustainability of amateur football in Upstate New York.

On the field, the Spins finished the inaugural regular season first and won the first UPL Final. The 2026 title followed the club’s first CDSL championship in 2025.

The next step is to keep building: a stronger matchday experience, lasting local partnerships, opportunities for committed players, and a club that people continue to feel part of.

The Grassroots Cup

A Matchday That Belongs to Everyone.

The Grassroots Cup grew into a focal point for the club’s community ambitions. The 2024 event brought more than 350 spectators to Lansing Park, and the following year nearly 500 gathered at Alumni Field. These occasions show what a local football club can create when the match is part of a wider gathering.

Supporters, volunteers and local partners turn the event into something larger than a result. That spirit remains central to how we want the club to grow.

A Historic Crest

Rooted in Cohoes.

Introduced in 2023, our crest draws on the city’s Victorian Gothic character. Its central silhouette honors the women and children who worked in the Cohoes mills.
