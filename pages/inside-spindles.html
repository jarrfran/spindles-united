Up the Spins

A Club for the Community.

Spindles United FC is an amateur soccer club based in Cohoes, New York. Our men’s side competes in the Upstate Premier League. We’re here to grow accessible, community-driven football in the Capital Region.

Contact the Club

Beyond the Pitch

Built Around People.

Our partnership with Cohoes Recreational Soccer began in 2023, supporting the Soccer Tots program for children ages four to six. Through volunteering, local partnerships and the Grassroots Cup, we bring the game closer to our neighbors.

A Historic Crest

Rooted in Cohoes.

Introduced in 2023, our crest draws on the city’s Victorian Gothic character. Its central silhouette honors the women and children who worked in the Cohoes mills.
