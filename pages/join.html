Before You Join

Your Questions,
Answered.

Get a feel for the commitment, then tell us about your playing background and availability.

Where do you train and play?

We train and play at Waterford High School. Away matches involve travel within the Capital Region.

How often does the team train?

Twice a week. Ask the club for the current days and times, and tell us about any availability constraints when you reach out.

Is there a player fee?

Yes. A basic player fee applies, consistent with player fees across the league. The club can explain the current arrangements before you commit.

What playing experience should I have?

We compete in the Upstate Premier League. Tell us your position and playing background so we can discuss whether the team is a good fit.

What happens after I submit interest?

Your message goes to the club through the contact form. We can discuss your experience, availability, and possible next steps. Submitting interest does not guarantee a roster place or playing time.

Can I ask questions before committing?

Of course. Use the Interest Form to ask about the team, training, or fitting football around your other commitments. You are starting a conversation, not committing to a season.
