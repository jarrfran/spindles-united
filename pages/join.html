Why Spindles

Amateur Level.
Professional Experience.

Spindles United represents Cohoes in the Upstate Premier League. We run the club with a professional standard, on the pitch and off it.

  • Stay Sharp All SummerCompetitive football that fits around college spring and fall seasons, or keeps you playing at a high level after college.
  • Play at a Real LevelOur squad mixes current and former college players with players who compete at that level. Across the UPL, that ranges from NCAA Division III to Division I.
  • Get SeenThe most followed club in the UPL and one of the most followed in upstate New York. Our players get real coverage online.

The Standard.

  • Commitment.

    We want players who train, not players who only show up on matchday.

  • Presence.

    Make yourself known in the squad. Being on the roster doesn’t make you part of it.

  • Communication.

    Keep coaches in the loop on your availability, early and often.

  • Performance.

    Deliver on the pitch. Respect the opportunity, or it goes to someone else.

Good to Know

Quick Answers.

Anything else, ask us through the Interest Form.

Can I talk to you before tryouts?

Yes. We’re happy to have a conversation beforehand about the level, the competition and anything else you want to know.

Is there an age requirement?

Yes. The UPL is an 18+ league, so you must be 18 by the start of the UPL season in mid-May.

Is there a player fee?

Yes. The player fee is consistent and competitive with player fees across the UPL.

Does sending the form get me on the team?

No. The form starts a conversation. Places are earned through tryouts, training and commitment, and they have to be earned again every season.
