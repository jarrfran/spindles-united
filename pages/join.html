Before You Reach Out

A Few Good
Questions.

Have something else in mind?
Ask us in the Interest Form.

Where do you train and play?

We train and play at Waterford High School. Away matches involve travel within the Capital Region.

How often does the team train?

We train twice a week. Contact the club for the current training days and times, and let us know about any availability constraints.

Is there a player fee?

Yes. There is a basic player fee consistent with player fees across the league. The club can explain the current fee and arrangements when you get in touch.

What level does Spindles play at?

Spindles United competes in the Upstate Premier League. Tell us about your playing background and preferred position so we can discuss the fit.

What is expected of me?

Commitment to training and matches, clear communication, a willingness to improve, and respect for everyone involved in the game. Team-first effort matters here.

Can I ask about joining before committing?

Absolutely. The Interest Form is a way to start a conversation. Share your questions and availability; submitting it does not commit you to joining.

Does submitting the form guarantee a place?

No. Roster places and playing time are not guaranteed. The club will discuss possible next steps after learning more about you.
