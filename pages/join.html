Good to Know

Quick Answers.

Anything else, ask us through the Interest Form.

What level do I need to be?

We compete in the Upstate Premier League. Tell us your position and playing background and we’ll talk about whether the team is a good fit.

Is there a player fee?

Yes. A basic player fee applies, in line with fees across the league. We’ll explain the details before you commit.

Am I guaranteed a spot?

Sending the form starts a conversation. It doesn’t guarantee a roster place or playing time.
